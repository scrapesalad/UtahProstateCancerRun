Ogden to Wheeler Historic Farm

Join Us from Weber County

Ogden and Weber County residents, join us for the Utah Prostate Cancer Run & Walk! Wheeler Historic Farm in Murray is approximately a 45-minute drive south via I-15.

Driving Directions

  • Take I-15 South from Ogden
  • Exit at 5300 South (Exit 302) in Murray
  • Head east on 5300 South
  • Turn south on 900 East
  • Wheeler Historic Farm is at 6351 S 900 E

Weber County Prostate Cancer Awareness

Weber County men face significant prostate cancer risks, with rates similar to the national average. Early detection saves lives - join us to raise awareness and support research that benefits men throughout Northern Utah.
