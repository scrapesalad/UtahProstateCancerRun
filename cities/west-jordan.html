West Jordan to Wheeler Historic Farm

Close to Home

West Jordan residents, the Utah Prostate Cancer Run & Walk is right in your neighborhood! Wheeler Historic Farm in Murray is just a 15-minute drive east - bring the whole family for a morning of fun and purpose.

Driving Directions

  • Head east on 7000 South or 7800 South
  • Continue east through Murray
  • Turn north on 900 East

West Jordan Community

West Jordan is one of Utah's fastest-growing cities with strong family values. By participating in the Utah Prostate Cancer Run & Walk, you're helping raise awareness about men's health issues that affect fathers, grandfathers, brothers, and sons in our community.
