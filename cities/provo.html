Join Us from Utah County

Provo and Utah County residents, join us for the Utah Prostate Cancer Run & Walk! Wheeler Historic Farm in Murray is approximately a 45-minute drive north via I-15.

Driving Directions

  • Take I-15 North from Provo
  • Exit at 5300 South (Exit 302) in Murray
  • Head east on 5300 South
  • Turn south on 900 East
  • Wheeler Historic Farm is at 6351 S 900 E

Utah County Prostate Cancer Awareness

Men in Utah County face the same prostate cancer risks as men statewide. With Provo's large university community and family-focused population, raising awareness about early detection is crucial. BYU students, faculty, and Provo families are all welcome to participate!
