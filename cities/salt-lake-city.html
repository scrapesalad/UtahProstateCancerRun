Join Us from Salt Lake City

Salt Lake City to Wheeler Historic Farm

Easy Access from Salt Lake City

Salt Lake City residents, join us for the Utah Prostate Cancer Run & Walk! Wheeler Historic Farm in Murray is just a 15-minute drive from downtown Salt Lake City via I-15 South.

Driving Directions

  • Take I-15 South from Salt Lake City
  • Exit at 5300 South (Exit 302)
  • Head east on 5300 South
  • Turn south on 900 East
  • Wheeler Historic Farm is on your right at 6351 S 900 E

Salt Lake County Prostate Cancer Statistics

In Salt Lake County, prostate cancer remains the most common cancer among men. Early detection through regular PSA screenings can significantly improve outcomes. Join us to raise awareness and funds for research that helps men across the Wasatch Front.
