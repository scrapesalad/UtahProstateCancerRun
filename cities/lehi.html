Lehi to Wheeler Historic Farm

Tech Corridor Meets Community Care

Lehi residents, join us for the Utah Prostate Cancer Run & Walk! Wheeler Historic Farm in Murray is approximately a 30-minute drive north via I-15 - a perfect Saturday morning adventure for your family.

Driving Directions

  • Take I-15 North from Lehi
  • Exit at 5300 South (Exit 302) in Murray
  • Head east on 5300 South
  • Turn south on 900 East
  • Wheeler Historic Farm is at 6351 S 900 E

Lehi & The Tech Corridor

Lehi, home to Thanksgiving Point and a booming tech industry, is known for its family-friendly community and innovation mindset. Bring that same spirit of innovation to men's health awareness - early detection and research are changing outcomes for prostate cancer patients.
