Draper to Wheeler Historic Farm

Silicon Slopes Joins the Fight

Draper residents, the Utah Prostate Cancer Run & Walk is right up the road! Wheeler Historic Farm in Murray is just a 10-minute drive north - perfect for tech professionals looking to start their Saturday with purpose.

Driving Directions

  • Head north on I-15 or Bangerter Highway
  • Exit at 5300 South or continue on surface streets
  • Head to 900 East in Murray
  • Wheeler Historic Farm is at 6351 S 900 E

Draper & Silicon Slopes Community

Draper, at the heart of Utah's Silicon Slopes tech corridor, is home to many hardworking professionals and families. Take a break from the screens and join the community in supporting prostate cancer awareness - a cause that affects men of all ages and backgrounds.
